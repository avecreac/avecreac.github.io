Coding Challenge 1

Exports and Imports of Goods in Indonesia

This chart shows Indonesia’s goods exports and imports in US dollars from 2000 to 2025. Both grew strongly over the period, with exports peaking at about $292 billion in 2022. Indonesia recorded a goods trade surplus in 21 of the 26 years, with deficits in 2012-2014 and 2018-2019. Exports are valued free on board (FOB) and imports include insurance and freight (CIF), so the two are not perfectly like-for-like.

Source: IMF, International Trade in Goods statistics.

Coding Challenge 2

Lithium Exports and EV Car Sales in Eastern and Southeastern Asia

This chart shows the relationship between lithium exports and electric vehicle (EV) car sales in Eastern and Southeastern Asia from 2010 to 2024. Lithium exports have grown significantly over the period, while EV car sales have also increased steadily. The chart highlights the correlation between these two trends, showing how rising lithium production has supported the growth of the EV market.

Source: UNCTAD Statistics, IEA
